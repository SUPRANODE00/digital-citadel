# Digital Citadel | Sovereign Intelligence & Petition Registry

A cryptographically secured, E-SIGN compliant digital citadel designed for the publication of speculative, theoretical, and artistic data streams under strict jurisdictional isolation and liability shields.

## Architecture
* **Gatekeeper Wall (`index.html` + `contract.js`):** Intercepts visitors with mandatory click-through terms, risk assumption checkboxes, and forensic metadata logging.
* **Signatory Registry (`signatories.md`):** Public ledger of voluntary petition supporters.
* **Cryptographic Attestation (`signatories.md.asc`):** PGP-signed verification proving administrative integrity and non-repudiable state updates.
